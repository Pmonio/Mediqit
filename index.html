FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before Mediqit launches.

Can I order from Mediqit now?

Not yet. Mediqit is preparing to launch, and orders are not open. Join the launch list to receive the opening catalogue and launch announcement.

When will Mediqit launch?

The launch date has not been announced. We’ll email the launch list when the date, opening catalogue and initial delivery coverage are confirmed.

Where will Mediqit deliver?

Our ambition is to serve customers across Nigeria. Initial delivery areas, costs and expected timings will be confirmed before orders open. Nationwide delivery is not yet confirmed.

Which products, brands and prices can I expect?

The planned range includes home monitors, first-aid supplies, thermometers and clinical equipment for students. Specific brands, models, prices and availability will be published in the opening catalogue.

How will I know what I’m buying?

We’re preparing product pages with brand and model details, specifications, package contents and compatible supplies. Confirmed sourcing and product-support information will be shared as it becomes available. You can ask product questions through our contact page.

Does Mediqit provide medical advice?

No. Mediqit is a health-commerce retailer and does not provide diagnosis or treatment. Speak with a qualified healthcare professional about symptoms, emergencies or treatment decisions.
