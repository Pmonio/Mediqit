PRIVACY NOTICE

Your information, handled clearly.

This notice explains how Mediqit Healthcare Solutions collects and uses information from people who visit our website or join our launch list.

Effective date: 11 October 2026  ·  Last updated: 16 October 2026

On this page Who we are Information we collect Student questionnaire How we use it Service providers Retention and security Your rights Contact us

Who we are

Mediqit Healthcare Solutions is an online health-commerce retailer. For the information described in this notice, Mediqit determines why and how it is used.

Information we collect

When you join our launch list, we collect:

  • Your email address.
  • Your required consent to receive launch emails.
  • Your optional choice about student-kit launch updates.
  • The date, time and version of the consent notice you accepted.
  • A one-way hash of your email address, used as a storage identifier.

We also process your IP address briefly to limit repeated submissions and protect the form from abuse. It is not included in your stored waitlist record.

Student questionnaire

Our optional questionnaire collects your institution, course, equipment needs and the challenges you face finding and buying equipment. No name or email is required. Please do not include personal health information. It is intended for adults aged 18 or older.

With your consent, we store responses privately with Vercel to research and plan our student product range. Responses may be processed outside Nigeria. We retain individual responses for up to 12 months, then delete them or retain only anonymised summaries. Completing the questionnaire does not subscribe you to marketing emails. We also record a response date and consent version, and briefly process IP addresses for abuse prevention without saving them in survey records.

A separate analytics event records successful survey completions without sending your answers to analytics. Contact hello@mediqit.com with privacy questions or requests; without contact details, identifying your particular response may require details you provide.

How and why we use it

We rely on your consent to send the communications you selected. We use your information to:

  • Send Mediqit launch-date, catalogue and delivery updates.
  • Send occasional product updates only when you separately request them.
  • Record and manage your communication choices.
  • Record whether you asked for medical-student equipment and clinical starter-kit updates.
  • Protect the waitlist form from spam, misuse and excessive requests.

We do not sell your email address or use it to send product updates unless you selected that option.

Website analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand aggregated website activity, such as page views, referral sources, general location, browser, operating system and device type. The service is designed not to use third-party cookies or associate analytics with an identifiable person. Our waitlist event records whether product updates were selected, but it does not send your email address to analytics.

Service providers and data location

We use Vercel to host this website, operate the waitlist function, store waitlist records in private storage and provide website analytics. These services process information on our behalf and may process it in countries outside Nigeria. We do not give third parties permission to use your waitlist information for their own marketing.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect our website, users or legal rights.

Retention and security

We keep waitlist information while it is needed for Mediqit's launch communications. We will remove it when you withdraw consent or request deletion, unless we must retain limited information to meet a legal obligation or document your communication preferences.

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect waitlist information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. Waitlist records are stored in private cloud storage. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable Nigerian data-protection law, you may ask us to:

  • Confirm whether we hold information about you and provide access to it.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Delete your information or restrict how it is used.
  • Object to certain processing or request a portable copy where applicable.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.

Withdrawing consent does not make earlier processing unlawful. You can unsubscribe through any marketing email or contact us directly. You may also raise a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Children's information

The launch list is intended for adults. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information. A parent or guardian who believes a child has joined the list should contact us so we can remove the information.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as Mediqit's services and data practices develop. We will publish the revised notice here and update the date above. Where a change materially affects how we use information already collected, we will provide an appropriate notice.
