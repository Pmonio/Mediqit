PRIVACY NOTICE

Your email, handled clearly.

When you join the Mediqit launch list, we collect your email address, the consent choices you make and the time those choices were recorded.

How we use it

We use your email to send launch updates. If you separately choose product updates, we may also send occasional information about Mediqit health essentials. We do not sell your email address.

Your choices

You may withdraw consent using the unsubscribe option included in Mediqit emails. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing completed before withdrawal.

Storage and retention

Waitlist records are stored securely and retained only as long as needed for launch communication and related legal obligations. Access is limited to people operating Mediqit's launch communications.

Questions

You can reply to any Mediqit email you receive to ask a privacy question or request removal from the waitlist.
