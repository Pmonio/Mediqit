Think beyond the meter itself.

Before choosing a meter, consider the ongoing availability and correct storage of its compatible consumables. Use the device only according to its instructions and professional guidance.

01 · COMPATIBILITY

Match the test strips

Test strips are model-specific. Confirm the exact compatible strip before purchasing or restocking.

02 · SUPPLY

Plan for replenishment

Consider strip pack sizes, lancets and realistic availability, not only the initial price of the meter.

03 · USE

Follow instructions

Correct storage, sample handling and device care matter. Follow the supplied manual and clinical guidance.

Common questions

Will strips be listed with compatible meters?

That is the plan. Compatibility will be stated clearly for products included in Mediqit's opening catalogue.

Does a glucometer provide a diagnosis?

No. It supplies measurements for monitoring. A qualified healthcare professional should interpret results in the context of your care.

What if a reading or symptom concerns me?

Follow the guidance given by your healthcare professional and seek appropriate medical care. Do not rely on a retail website for diagnosis or emergency advice.
